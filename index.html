MY HAIR STORY

Eight years in the making.

In 2018 I went natural. I thought it would be easy — it wasn't.

From 2018 to 2026, I battled dryness, breakage, products that promised everything and delivered nothing.

I tried everything. My bathroom was full of half-used jars, but my hair was still thirsty.

I was tired of my hair breaking more than it was growing.

I wanted something thick enough to seal, but soft enough to melt. Something natural that would actually stay.

That's our Shea Avo Butter.

When I tried it on my own hair for the first time, I knew. My hair was softer. It stayed moisturized for days, not hours. The hydration was sealed in.

This isn't just a butter. It's the end of my eight-year search — and the beginning of yours.

Now I share it for every woman who just wants a product that finally works.
